Completion score = completed ÷ generated × 100. Every unfinished car lowers the score, including cars unable to enter. Compare controllers on identical demand and duration.

Time in system includes every generated car, from scheduled departure until arrival or cutoff, including entry backlog. Compare identical demand and duration; lower is better. Completed-only travel and wait exclude unfinished cars and cannot rank overall performance.
